## The word

> corridance (n.) — the awkward mirrored sidestep two strangers do while trying to pass each other in a narrow hallway.

*Corridance* names the little dance nobody choreographed: two people meet in a narrow hallway, both step the same way, both correct, both step the same way again — until one of them finally laughs and lets the other through. Everybody has done it; English just never gave it a name.
